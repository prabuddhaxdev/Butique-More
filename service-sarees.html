Customization Options

Every saree we paint is a blank canvas. We work closely with you to ensure the final piece perfectly reflects your aesthetic, whether it's for a wedding, a special event, or an heirloom piece.

  • Fabric Choices: Pure Silk, Organza, Chiffon, Georgette, Cotton Silk. (You can also provide your own fabric)
  • Design Styles: Traditional motifs, contemporary florals, abstract art, Madhubani inspired, geometric patterns.
  • Color Themes: Fully customizable palettes based on your preferences.
  • Painting Techniques: Fabric acrylics, watercolor effects, metallic accents.
  • Timeline: Typically 4-6 weeks for design, painting, and delivery.
